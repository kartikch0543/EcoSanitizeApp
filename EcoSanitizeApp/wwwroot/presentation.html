The Urban Sanitation Crisis

Why Cities Struggle with Waste Management

Mixed Waste Contamination
Organic food waste, plastics, industrial chemicals, and biomedical items are collected together, ruining recycling opportunities and creating toxic landfill fires.
Unmonitored Collection Delays
Municipal vehicles lack centralized dispatching, leaving commercial bulk waste and emergency hazardous bins sitting on streets for days without tracking.
Unverified Public Cleanliness
High-traffic public hubs like bus stands, markets, and clinic reception areas lack quantitative inspection protocols and certified disinfectant chemical verification.
Citizen Helplessness
Residents observing open garbage dumping or overflowing public bins have no direct, photo-verified civic reporting channel with tracked departmental resolution.
Unified Civic Platform

The Solution: EcoSanitize Command Center

1. Standardized 5-Stream Segregation
Rigorous classification into Organic, Recyclable, Hazardous, Sanitary, and E-Waste streams with container color codes and treatment standards.
2. Real-Time Disposal Tracking Queue
Triage lifecycle managing bulk requests from Pending through In-Transit vehicle dispatch to verified certified Disposal.
3. Facility Sanitization Auditing
Quantitative 1-5 hygiene inspection ratings with disinfectant chemical logging and automated corrective follow-up alerts.
4. Citizen Grievance Portal & Analytics
Photo evidence ticket submission, ward-wise resolution tracking, live telemetry charts, and automated CSV/PDF export.

Pillar 1: Waste Classification

Standardized 5-Stream Segregation

Organic
Green Bin | Haz: 1/10
Food scraps, wet waste, vegetable peelings. Channeled for aerobic composting and biogas.
Recyclable
Blue Bin | Haz: 2/10
Paper, corrugated cartons, plastics, glass, metals. Sent to recovery plants.
Hazardous
Red Bin | Haz: 8/10
Chemical reagents, paints, industrial solvents. Encapsulated in containment facilities.
Sanitary
Yellow Bin | Haz: 7/10
Clinic swabs, biohazard disposables, pads. High-temperature medical autoclaving.
E-Waste
Orange Bin | Haz: 6/10
Circuit boards, spent batteries, old electronics. Dismantled for precious metal recovery.
Pillar 2: Logistics Lifecycle

Disposal Tracking & Dispatch Queue

1. Pickup Request Submission
Bulk generators (markets, laboratories, hospitals) submit pickup requests specifying location, estimated weight in kg, category, and urgency level (Low, Medium, High, Emergency).
2. Live Queue & Stream Filtering
Supervisors view all requests in a responsive data table, filterable by waste stream. Urgent and emergency items are highlighted with clear visual indicators.
3. Dispatch & In-Transit Tracking
Supervisors assign a municipal collector or vehicle unit (e.g. 'Hazmat Unit 01') and transition status to 'In-Transit' with one click.
4. Verified Disposal Closure
Once the materials arrive at the authorized recycling or composting facility, the record is transitioned to 'Disposed', creating a verified audit trail.
Pillar 3: Public Hygiene

Facility Sanitization Audits

Quantitative Cleanliness Scores
Replaces subjective reviews with a strict 1 to 5 numerical inspection score. Displays transparent ratios like '5 / 5' or '2 / 5' with subtle color coding for failing scores.
Disinfectant Chemical Verification
Audits verify and record certified chemical agents used on-site (such as Sodium Hypochlorite 1% solution or Quaternary Ammonium Compounds) rather than unverified washing.
Automated Corrective Action Trigger
Scores <= 2 automatically trigger 'Requires Follow-Up = Yes' and mark the facility as 'Needs Attention', scheduling mandatory deep cleaning re-inspection.
Clean Municipal Audit Ledger
Inspection records are presented in a clean, humanized municipal ledger showing area name, inspector, disinfectant, rating, and final pass/attention outcome.
Pillar 4: Citizen Engagement

Citizen Grievance Redressal with Photo Proof

1. Issue Identification
Citizens identify civic sanitation hazards such as overflowing street bins, illegal open dumping, clogged storm drains, or missed household collections.
2. Photo Upload & Description
Citizens file a ticket via the portal, attaching photographic evidence stored securely on the server with timestamp and location details.
3. Ward-Wise Automated Triage
Grievance records are automatically organized by municipal Ward (e.g. Ward 04, Ward 12), enabling immediate departmental accountability.
4. Site Clearance & Resolution Notes
Field sanitation teams clear the site, update status to 'Resolved', and record official resolution notes visible to administrators.
Pillar 5: Field Operations

Workforce Management & Collection Timetables

Worker Directory
Tracks employee records with ID, full name, zone, and phone contact to maintain complete field crew visibility.
Shift Duty Toggle
Allows supervisors to update worker status (Active, On-Duty, On-Leave) with a single click to reflect daily staffing.
Ward Collection Timetables
Organizes recurring route schedules by Ward Zone, Day of the Week, and Shift Time (e.g. Morning 06:30 AM).
Stream-Specific Truck Mapping
Allocates designated trucks to specific streams (e.g. Wet Organic on Mon/Thu, Dry Recyclable on Tue/Fri) to prevent mixing.
Municipal Intelligence

Visual Telemetry & Exportable Reports

Daily Collection Trend (Bar Graph)
Visualizes daily tonnage collected over the last 7 days to identify peak collection days and optimize vehicle fuel and crew allocation.
Stream Composition (Doughnut Chart)
Illustrates the exact ratio of Organic, Recyclable, Hazardous, Sanitary, and E-Waste collected across the municipality.
Citizen Grievances (Horizontal Bar Chart)
Highlights primary complaint types (Overflowing Bins, Missed Pickups, Drainage) to guide municipal budget allocation.
Instant Data Exports (CSV & PDF)
1-click server-side CSV Spreadsheet exports (Disposal Logs, Complaints) and an official Printable PDF Audit Report for municipal review.
